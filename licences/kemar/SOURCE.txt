MIT KEMAR HRTF measurements, "compact" set (128-tap HRIRs, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit big-endian, L/R interleaved,
azimuth 0..180 = source on the right; mirror for the left side).
Downloaded 2026-10-18 from the official page: https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html
(file https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR/compact.tar.Z)
